Sourced 2026-11-20 for the content lead (queue task qitem-20261120143701-7140f031657b5db2, pack 2 of 12). No pack file was touched.

**Numbering:** question numbers are the position in `tool/packs/hr-znanost/hr-znanost.json` (1 to 130) as of 2026-11-20.

**Size plan:** 55 new × ~40 KB = about 2.2 MB at 640 px, well inside the limit. Download scaled Commons thumbnails (standard widths such as 640 or 1280), not the originals.

## How it was checked

- Each file's licence, author, size and mime came from the Commons API. Only public domain (incl. PD-USGov), CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA were kept. All are at least 640 px wide, JPG/PNG, no SVG.
- I re-checked 19 of the 55 (every third) against the live API: licence and width matched in all 19.
- **Nobody opened the images.** Descriptions and metadata only. Look at each when wiring it in, especially the weak fits named in "Preskočeno / sumnje".
- Credit is required for CC BY and CC BY-SA files (Q1 is CC BY-SA 3.0 de).
- Only 6 questions need rewording (the picture would give away the answer): Q4 Jupiter, Q5 Saturn, Q23 Mars, Q64 Neptune, Q66 Ganymede, Q104 olm. These fit within the cap of ~40 "Tko je na slici" rewordings.

- Odbijeno zbog licence: Iron i Lead electrolytic (Alchemist-hp) su FAL (Free Art License), ne CC; zato nema slike za pitanja 2 i 85.

- Nepotpuno: pitanja 13, 25, 71, 102, 2, 85 nemaju sliku; nisu tražene dodatne alternative.
- Slike koje otkrivaju odgovor (označeno "da"): 4, 5, 23, 64, 66, 104. Kod 66 (Ganimed) slika sama ne razlikuje mjesec bez opisa; ovisi o formulaciji.
- Pitanje 100 (Hooke): slika je Hookeov crtež pluta iz Micrographije, bez imena autora na samoj slici; nije portret. Slika 59 (Boyleova pumpa) i 53 (Bellov telefon) djelomično upućuju na odgovor, ali ga ne navode.
- Pitanje 39: opis datoteke (zalazak sunca s ISS-a) ne spominje izričito ozonski sloj; slika prikazuje slojevitu atmosferu pri horizontu. Provjeriti prije uporabe.

- Pitanje 42: slika je stakleni prizma, ne cijeli spektar; za crvenu boju bolja bi bila slika spektra, nije tražena.
- Pitanje 107: slika je kraška stijena u Italiji (Karst, IT), ne hrvatski krš.
