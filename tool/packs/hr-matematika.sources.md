# hr-matematika: sources

Checked online on 2026-11-16, read-only (hr/en.wikipedia). One line per question, in pack order: the fact as found,
then the page; "izračun" means the answer is plain arithmetic, checked by a script that evaluates each one. Question
wording is ours; nothing is copied from quiz sites (see SOURCES-research.md). The well-known puzzles (bat and ball,
five machines, lily pads) are retold in our own words.

Pictures: every SVG in img/ was drawn for this project (generated by script, to scale) and is ours to publish.

Least sure: the five symmetry axes of the star rest on "dihedral group of order 10" (en.wikipedia), not a sentence
that says five; Bilinski "rediscovered" the rhombic dodecahedron of the second kind (Cowley drew it in 1752); India as
the home of the first rules for zero rests on Brahmagupta being Indian.

Task 44 (2026-11-17): made easier for a pub-quiz audience. 55 of the hardest questions (textbook problems, multi-step arithmetic, specialist history) were removed and 45 pub-level questions added (Q69–Q113); the rest are unchanged and renumbered.
