question, in pack order: the fact as found, then the page. Question wording is ours; nothing is copied from quiz
sites (see SOURCES-research.md). Meme images are mostly copyrighted and the repo is public, so the pictures (added 2026-11-20, list at the end)
show the person, animal, place or object behind a meme, never the meme itself.

Left out: Pepe, Ugandan Knuckles, Harambe, Star Wars Kid, Ken Lee, political memes, anything cruel to a private
person; view counts and "most popular" claims. No Croatian meme could be verified on a citable page, so the regional
ones are O-Zone, Epic Sax Guy, Trololo, Hide the Pain Harold and Pedro.

(the page names dwarfism and an underbite; only dwarfism is offered); show titles shortened to fit ("The Real
Housewives").
