Sourced 2026-11-20 for the content lead (queue task qitem-20261120143701-7140f031657b5db2, pack 6 of 12). No pack file was touched.

**Numbering:** question numbers are the position in `tool/packs/hr-ljudsko-tijelo/hr-ljudsko-tijelo.json` (1 to 106) as of 2026-11-20.

**Count:** 44 new pictures + 10 questions that already have one = 54 of 106 = 51 % (target 53).

**Size plan:** 44 new × ~80 KB = about 3.5 MB. Q42 (fundus) is about 16000×16000 px: download a scaled thumbnail.

## How it was checked

- Each file's licence, author, size and mime came from the Commons API. Only PD, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA were kept. All are at least 640 px wide, JPG/PNG, no SVG.
- I re-checked 15 of the 44 against the live API: licence and width matched in all 15.
- **Nobody opened the images.** I could not check for embedded caption or label text. Look by eye before use: Q20 (DK ATLAS), Q51 (Basque-labelled Gray plate), Q57, Q90 (3D pepsin model; author is only "own work", check the uploader), Q95.
- **29 questions are marked for rewording** ("Što je na slici?" style, with Croatian wording suggested). Only 2 are portraits (Q80 Broca, Q100 Dalton). Q58 (karyotype: chromosomes can be counted) and Q82 (lobes visible) partly give the answer away.
- Possibly sensitive: Q16 (bull gallbladder), Q50 (museum appendix specimen), Q79 (human brain in a jar); Q42 (fundus) and Q95 (eardrum) are anonymous medical photos. Q64 and Q97 show parts of a living person: check no face is visible.
- Not verified: Q47 (milk teeth, content known only from title and description) and Q50.
- Most Gray's Anatomy plates are under 640 px wide and were dropped. That is why many anatomy questions have no picture (aorta, vena cava, nephron, Achilles tendon, gluteus, larynx, ear and others); details in "Preskočeno / sumnje".
- Credit is required for CC BY and CC BY-SA files.
