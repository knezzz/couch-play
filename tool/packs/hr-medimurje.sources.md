## Dropped from the first version, and why

- "Najsjevernija županija": no source says it in those words. Replaced by the northernmost point (Q11).
- Goričan border crossing, Dubravsko jezero, Toplice Sveti Martin, Ludbreg, registration plate ČK, Muzej Međimurja: true but too easy for locals.
- Words not found as Međimurje usage, or found with a different local form: fruštuk (Čakovec says zajtrek), pinezi
  (Čakovec says penezi), cuker (the Dekanovec entry is cukur, meaning slatkiš), hiža, vura, popevka, gda, zdenec, kmica, štenge,
  dimo, pajdaš, kaj. Only words with an entry in the Međimurje dictionary remain.
- "Opsada Sigeta" epic question: replaced by Nikola Zrinski's death in Kuršanečki lug (Q18), which is more local.
