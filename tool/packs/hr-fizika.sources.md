# hr-fizika: sources

Checked online on 2026-11-16, read-only (hr/en.wikipedia, enciklopedija.hr). One line per question, in pack order:
the fact as found, then the page; "izračun" means the answer is plain arithmetic, checked by a script that evaluates
each one. Question wording is ours; nothing is copied from quiz sites (see SOURCES-research.md). Facts already asked
in hr-znanost and hr-opce-znanje (SI-unit namesakes, Röntgen, Doppler, Tesla, Mohorovičić, Vrančić) are left out.

Pictures: every SVG in img/ was drawn for this project (generated by script, to scale; IEC circuit symbols) and is
ours to publish.

Least sure: Supek as "one of the founders" of Institut Ruđer Bošković (the page's wording); Croatian textbook terms
("tromost", "jakost leće", "rasipne leće") written from usage, not checked against a textbook; the short-circuited
bulb picture is correct but easy to misread.
