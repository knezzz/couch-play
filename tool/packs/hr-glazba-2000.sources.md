Checked online on 2026-11-21 (en/hr.wikipedia raw page text; one row uses enciklopedija.hr). One line per question,
in pack order: two sources, each the page and the passage it was checked against (wiki markup stripped). Question
wording is ours; nothing is copied from quiz, TV-quiz or quiz-site questions. Facts that can change carry a year in
the question ("do 2024.", "2025."). Living people: public work only.

Split with hr-pop-kultura (quiz-writer, agreed 2026-11-21): no Croatian Eurovision entries or results, no Dora, no
Croatian festivals, no Croatian music before 2000; those are in hr-pop-kultura.
Left out because another pack asks it: the first video to a billion YouTube views (Gangnam Style, hr-memeovi), who
sang Gangnam Style (hr-memeovi names Psy), the year YouTube started (hr-memeovi, hr-koliko-godine: first video 2005).
Also left out: Jelena Rozga in Magazin (hr-pop-kultura asks Danijela and Magazin). The 2005, 2019 and 2021 Eurovision
questions ask the singer or band, not the country, because the winner hosts the next contest and hr-pop-kultura asks
the host city or country of 2006, 2021 and 2022.
Not used: Eurovision 2007, 2008, 2016, 2018, 2022 winners (political context); Shazam and Gibonni's "Mirakul" (no
source found).
