## How it was checked

- Each file's licence, author, size and mime came from the Commons API. Only PD (Q17 NPS; Q26, Q33, Q54 NASA), CC0 (Q63, Q90), CC BY and CC BY-SA were kept. All are JPG, at least 1024 px wide.
- I re-checked 14 of the 41 against the live API: licence and width matched in all 14.
- **Nobody opened the images.** Whether a picture shows what its file title says is unchecked. Look at each when wiring it in.
- Only Q12 is marked for rewording (Budapest panorama while the question names Budimpešta: "Koja je ovo rijeka?"). On the capital-city questions the picture shows the answer city's landmark, so you may prefer "Koji je ovo grad?" wording there; they are marked "ne" because the picture shows what is asked about without naming it.
- Picture problems: Q58 Wellington is a 5:1 panorama (5408×1061), and Q9 Bern and Q41 Baikal are also very wide: crop or replace. Q71 is Catedrales de Tara (Atacama region, not labelled as the desert itself). Q82 relies on the file title for "Nicosia".
- Not searched: capitals without a distinctive landmark, straits and canals, history questions.
- Credit is required for CC BY and CC BY-SA files.
