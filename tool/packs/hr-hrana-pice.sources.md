# hr-hrana-pice: sources

Checked online on 2026-11-20, read-only (en/hr.wikipedia raw page text). One line per question, in pack order: the fact
as found, then the page. Question wording is ours; topics and style come from pub-quiz food rounds, nothing is copied
from quiz, TV-quiz or quiz-site questions. 49 of 99 questions have a photo (list at the end): Commons files with a
free licence, resized to 800 px WebP, chosen so the photo shows the thing asked about but not the answer (dropped:
Prošek bottles labelled "Prošek", a Pošip bottle labelled "Blato", haggis wrapped in tartan). Food items already asked in hr-opce-znanje
(tiramisu, ouzo, ementaler, gorgonzola, paella, šafran, julienne, kimči, vanilija, sous-chef) and lumblija, Dingač
and arambašići from other packs are left out.

After the fact-check (2026-11-20) 14 questions were dropped: 11 that repeated a question in another hr-* pack
(schnitzel, humus, pesto, guacamole, paški sir, soparnik, Neretva mandarina, maraschino, prošek, cappuccino, umami),
al dente (near-duplicate), and two whose name gave the answer away (mozzarella di bufala, pasterizacija). Numbers
below and the photo file names follow the new order.

Least sure: 54 (Welschriesling is the article's main name and the page lists Austria among its regions, but it does
not say in one sentence "called Welschriesling in Austria"); 8 (en.wiki says "even the national dish", citing
scotland.org); 29 (ajvar is mainly peppers, often with eggplant; eggplant is not among the choices); 32 (the
2015 first registration is from the hr.wiki list, which cites the ministry); 55 (asks the wine's colour, not a
ranking); 52 (hr.wiki's second meaning of bevanda is wine mixed with water; it does not say red wine, so the question
does not either). Photo 15: the chocolate seal on the cake carries the hotel's name, small and hard to read on a TV;
the fact-checker found nothing readable at sheet size.
