# hr-film-glazba: picture sourcing

Sourced 2026-11-20 for the content lead (queue task qitem-20261120143701-7140f031657b5db2, pack 3 of 12). No pack file was touched.

**Numbering:** question numbers are the position in `tool/packs/hr-film-glazba/hr-film-glazba.json` (1 to 130) as of 2026-11-20.

**Count:** 50 new pictures + 14 questions that already have one = 64 of 130 = 49 %. One short of 50 % (65). Film questions are the hardest to illustrate (no stills, posters or covers), so most pictures here show instruments, buildings, places and historic portraits.

**Size plan:** 50 new × ~40 KB = about 2 MB at 640 px. Q65 (8985 px wide) and Q107 (7398 px wide) are very large: download scaled Commons thumbnails. Q21 is exactly 640×480 (borderline).

## How it was checked

- Each file's licence, author, size and mime came from the Commons API. Only PD, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA were kept. All are JPG, at least 640 px wide.
- I re-checked 17 of the 50 (every third) against the live API: licence and width matched in all 17.
- **Nobody opened the images.** Descriptions and metadata only. Look at each when wiring it in.
- Only 2 questions are marked for rewording (Q12 Hendrix, Q77 Méliès). Q77's Commons PD tag (a 1938 magazine photo) was not verified independently: check before use.
- Weak fits, details in "Preskočeno / sumnje": the picture gives away or hints at the answer in Q26, Q41, Q61, Q63, Q64, Q70, Q72, Q114; the picture only loosely relates in Q11 (Mariinsky), Q13 (Battersea power station is from the album "Animals", not "Dark Side"), Q81 (Casablanca was not filmed there), Q105 (Dresden; premiere hall not confirmed).
- Not searched (rate limits and time): most actor/character questions and the Croatian ones (102-104, 106, 108-111, 113). Those are the first place to look if the writer wants the last picture.
