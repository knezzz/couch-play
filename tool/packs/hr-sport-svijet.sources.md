# hr-sport-svijet: sources

Checked online on 2026-11-21, read-only (en.wikipedia raw page text). One line per question, in pack order: the fact
as found on the page, then the page. Question wording is ours; topics, style and difficulty come from pub-quiz sport
rounds, nothing is copied from quiz, TV-quiz or quiz-site questions. Stable facts only: rules, history, closed careers
and past events with a year; no "current champion" or records an active athlete can still change.

Left out because another pack already asks them: players per team in football, rugby union, water polo, ice hockey and
baseball; marathon length; darts 180; Olympic pool and track lengths; volleyball net height; tennis court length;
18 holes and bogey in golf; caddie; Olympic ring colours; 1896 Athens; 1930 Uruguay; FIFA 1904; IOC 1894; 776 BC;
Wimbledon's grass; slam dunk; bowling pins (hr-koliko-opcenito, hr-opce-znanje); snooker's 15 reds (hr-koliko-opcenito
counts the balls); Kanō Jigorō and judo (hr-opce-znanje); the marathon's name from the battle (it would be answered by
the Boston and Bikila questions). Croatian sport stays in hr-kultura-sport.

Pictures (list at the end): Commons files, licence read on each file page, free in the EU; ~800 px WebP. No logos,
brands or Olympic rings in shot (dropped: Wilson/NFL ball, Everlast and Bail gloves, Kempa handball, Gymnova pommel
horse, Hahnenkamm start sign, the Coliseum gate with the rings). No photo of the answer place (dropped: Olympia for
the flame, St Andrews for "home of golf", the Thames for the Boat Race). US-only public-domain photos dropped (Owens
1936 Acme, Bannister, Chamberlain, Babe Ruth, Naismith, Chamonix rink). 41 and 79 show the person who is the answer,
so they are worded "who is on the photo".

After the fact-check (2026-11-21): Q39 (Rugby School, the answer was the sport's name) was dropped, so questions
40-100 moved up by one and the photos were renamed to match. Q12 (birdie, a mirror of hr-opce-znanje Q61) now asks
the 14-club limit. Q27 has a new photo (1912 bout; the old one had Nike/Puma marks), Q95 (was Q96) is cropped (no sponsor
boards), Q35 has no photo (photographer without a death year). Text fixes: Q4, Q7, Q8, Q15, Q41 (was Q42), Q70 (was Q71), Q93 (was Q94);
choices: Q63 (was Q64) U Kolkati for U Sydneyju, Q97 (was Q98) U Seulu for U Londonu.
