2. Koliko pari moždinskih (spinalnih) živaca ima čovjek? = 31 (from hr-ljudsko-tijelo Q23): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinal_nerve (states: 31 pairs)
3. Koliko kralježaka ima vratni dio kralježnice? = 7 (from hr-ljudsko-tijelo Q24): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cervical_vertebrae (states: seven)
4. Koliko pari rebara obično ima čovjek? = 12 (from hr-ljudsko-tijelo Q25): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rib_cage (states: 12 pairs)
5. Koliko mliječnih zuba obično imaju djeca? = 20 (from hr-ljudsko-tijelo Q47): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deciduous_teeth (states: a total of twenty teeth)
6. Koliko kromosoma ima većina stanica ljudskog tijela? = 46 (from hr-ljudsko-tijelo Q58): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karyotype (states: 46)
7. Koliko kostiju ima ljudska šaka sa zapešćem? = 27 (from hr-ljudsko-tijelo Q73): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hand (states: 27 bones)
8. Koliko režnjeva ima desno plućno krilo? = 3 (from hr-ljudsko-tijelo Q82): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lung (states: three lobes)
9. Koliko otprilike tjedana traje trudnoća, računajući od posljednje menstruacije? = 40 tjedana (from hr-ljudsko-tijelo Q86): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pregnancy (states: 40 weeks)
10. Pri kojoj temperaturi voda vrije na razini mora? = 100 °C (from hr-znanost Q8): water boils at 100 °C (sea level): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boiling_point
11. Koliko kostiju ima tijelo odraslog čovjeka? = 206 (from hr-znanost Q9): decreases to around 206 bones by adulthood: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_skeleton
12. Koliki je pH čiste vode pri 25 °C? = 7 (from hr-znanost Q20): pH of 7 at 25 °C are neutral, the same as pure water: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PH
13. Koliko otprilike treba svjetlosti da sa Sunca stigne do Zemlje? = 8 minuta (from hr-znanost Q24): light travels 1 au in slightly more than 8 minutes 19 seconds: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomical_unit
14. Koliko zuba obično ima odrasla osoba, s umnjacima? = 32 (from hr-znanost Q47): 32 permanent (adult) teeth: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_tooth
15. Otprilike svakih koliko se godina Halleyev komet vraća u Zemljinu blizinu? = 76 godina (from hr-znanost Q70): Halley's orbital period has varied between 74 and 80 years: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halley's_Comet
16. Koliko puta naraste kinetička energija tijela kad mu se brzina udvostruči? = 4 (from hr-fizika Q14): an object doubling its speed has four times as much kinetic energy: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetic_energy
17. Koliko je puta, približno, težina tijela na Mjesecu manja nego na Zemlji? = 6 (from hr-fizika Q80): Its surface gravity is about one-sixth of Earth's: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon
18. Grmljavinu čujemo 3 s nakon munje. Koliko je daleko munja ako zvuk prijeđe 340 m/s? = 1020 m (from hr-fizika Q101): izračun (s = v·t)
19. Zvuk frekvencije 170 Hz širi se brzinom 340 m/s. Kolika mu je valna duljina? = 2 m (from hr-fizika Q103): izračun (λ = v/f)
20. Koliko je ukupno točkica na svih šest strana igraće kocke? = 21 (from hr-matematika Q43): izračun
21. Koliko kvadratnih centimetara ima jedan kvadratni metar? = 10000 cm² (from hr-matematika Q44): izračun
22. Koliko litara stane u jedan kubni metar? = 1000 L (from hr-matematika Q45): izračun
23. Kocki udvostručimo brid. Koliko joj se puta poveća obujam? = 8 (from hr-matematika Q46): izračun
24. Koji je najmanji savršeni broj, jednak zbroju svojih djelitelja? = 6 (from hr-matematika Q92): 6 is the smallest perfect number; the next is 28: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_number
25. Koliki je zbroj točkica na suprotnim stranama igraće kocke? = 7 (from hr-matematika Q97): opposite sides all add up to seven, as in modern dice: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dice
26. Koliko bridova ima kocka? = 12 (from hr-matematika Q98): It has eight vertices and twelve straight edges: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cube
27. Koliko ima pravilnih (Platonovih) tijela? = 5 (from hr-matematika Q99): There are only five such polyhedra: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platonic_solid
28. Koliko strana (ploha) ima ikosaedar? = 20 (from hr-matematika Q100): icosahedron: twenty triangular faces: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platonic_solid
29. Koliki je zbroj prirodnih brojeva od 1 do 100, koji je kao dječak brzo izračunao Gauss? = 5050 (from hr-matematika Q103): sum the numbers from 1 to 100 ... the correct answer of 5050: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gauss
30. Koliko je „problema tisućljeća” 2000. objavio Clayev matematički institut? = 7 (from hr-matematika Q114): The Millennium Prize Problems are seven ... in 2000: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millennium_Prize_Problems
