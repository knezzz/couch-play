# hr-zivotinje: sources

Checked online on 2026-11-21, read-only (en/hr.wikipedia raw page text and Hrvatski jezični portal, hjp.znanje.hr). One
line per question, in pack order: the fact as found, then the page. Question wording is ours; topics and style come
from pub-quiz animal rounds, nothing is copied from quiz, TV-quiz or quiz-site questions. 51 of 99 questions have a
photo (list at the end): Commons files with a free licence (template read on the file page), resized to 800 px WebP,
chosen so the photo shows the animal asked about but not the answer.

Animal facts already asked in other packs are left out (blue whale, giraffe, cheetah, ostrich size, dromedary, koala,
octopus hearts, bee honey, foal, dove, insect legs, ruminant stomach, bats flying, platypus, olm, griffon vulture on
Cres, bottlenose dolphin, lynx, loggerhead, monk seal, wolf, the Đakovo stud farm, and the Blef priroda facts). Animals
that answer another pack's question get no photo here (bear, horse, dog, cat, elephant, dolphin, fox, swan, seahorse).
