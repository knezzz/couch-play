# hr-pop-kultura: sources

Checked online on 2026-11-21, read-only (hr/en.wikipedia raw page text). One
line per question, in pack order: the fact as found on the page (quoted or as the infobox gives it), then the page(s).
Question wording is ours; topics, style and difficulty come from pub-quiz pop-culture rounds, nothing is copied from
quiz, TV-quiz or quiz-site questions. Questions with a number, date or "first" as the answer have two sources.
Facts that can change carry a year in the wording ("od 2000.", "2019. preuzeo", "od 2023.").

Split with hr-glazba-2000 (party-writer, agreed 2026-11-21): this pack keeps all Croatian Eurovision entries and
results (any year), Dora, Croatian festivals as events, and Croatian pop/rock before 2000; post-2000 Croatian albums,
hits and band facts outside Eurovision are in hr-glazba-2000.

Left out because another pack already asks them (hr-film-glazba, hr-kultura-sport): Riva's home town, Bijelo dugme,
Štulić/Azra, Prljavo kazalište's neighbourhood, Pula film festival year, Animafest year, Baltazar's creator, Dragić,
Hlapić, Brešan's "Kako je počeo rat", "Jedan dan" 1968, Lustig, Višnjić, "Bitka na Neretvi", Smoje as author of
"Naše malo misto", Golik as director of "Tko pjeva zlo ne misli", Lisinski's opera "Porin" (so no question on the Porin
award), Dora Pejačević (asked three times elsewhere, so not as Dora's namesake). Band-leader questions for Haustor,
Film and Parni valjak are left out: with hr-film-glazba Q102 (Štulić -> Azra, choices Film/Azra/Parni valjak/Haustor)
they would answer it by elimination. Poljud (hr-kultura-sport Q116 photo) is not named for Ultra Europe.

No pictures: festival venues and town views would show the answer of another question here (Prokurative, Jarun,
Motovun, Korzo, Varaždin) or in hr-kultura-sport (Poljud, Maksimir); stage photos carry sponsor walls; film stills,
posters, covers and comic panels are not free.

After the fact-check (2026-11-21): Latinica dropped (the show is named after its host), so later questions moved
up by one. Reworded: Q4 (INmusic on Jarun "od 2007."), Q29 (Milijunaš host since the 2019 return), Q30 ("na Euroviziji"),
Q54 (the Silver Lion went to the episode "Maestro Koko", not the series), Q60 (Cinehill without "istarski"), Q65 (short
answer "Izvjestan pogled"), Q69 (Lisac's first studio album, music by Metikoš), Q77 (where the parade ends), Q94 (no
"za RTL", which answered Q51), Q99 ("mediji", "do 2009."). Choices: Q60 Filmski brijeg for Motofest, Q77 Na Kantridi
for Na Delti. Q39, Q57 and Q61 now ask the song instead of the host city or country, and Q52 no longer names Kijev:
host cities answer hr-glazba-2000's "who won the year before" questions.
