# hr-psihologija: sources

Checked online on 2026-11-16, read-only (hr/en.wikipedia, enciklopedija.hr, Proleksis, HBL, the FFZG psychology
department history page). One line per question, in pack order: the fact as found, then the page. Question wording is
ours; nothing is copied from quiz sites (see SOURCES-research.md). Established findings and terms only; pop myths
(10% of the brain, learning styles, left/right-brain types, MBTI, Stockholm syndrome) left out.

Least sure: some Croatian term forms not found on a Croatian page ("efekt puke izloženosti", "efekt koktel-zabave",
"efekt Zeigarnik"); Kübler-Ross's stages are asked as the model's content, not as a proven finding; the Stanford
prison experiment is asked only for what happened (who ran it, how long).
