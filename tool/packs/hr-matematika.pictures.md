# hr-matematika: picture sourcing

Sourced 2026-11-20 for the content lead (queue task qitem-20261120143701-7140f031657b5db2, pack 10 of 12). No pack file was touched.

**This pack does not suit pictures.** Most questions are arithmetic, definitions or word problems, and a picture would either add nothing or give the answer away. I did not pad it to 50 %.

**Numbering:** question numbers are the position in `tool/packs/hr-matematika/hr-matematika.json` (1 to 113) as of 2026-11-20.

**Count:** Part A: 15 pictures for existing questions (Q26, 38, 39, 40, 43, 55, 56, 58, 59, 61, 62, 64, 66, 68, 99) + 4 questions that already have one = 19 of 113 = 17 %. Part B: 8 proposed NEW picture questions (dodecahedron, octahedron, tangram, abacus, slide rule, Sierpiński triangle, Mandelbrot set, Königsberg plan), each with four choices and a source. With all of B: 27 picture questions of 121.

**Your call:** the 4 existing pictures are generated diagrams (percent, fractions, symmetry). Generated diagrams would fit this pack better than Commons photos: for example angles, a triangle, a coordinate grid, a bar chart. That is your team's, not mine.

## How it was checked

- Each file's licence, author, size and mime came from the Commons API. Only PD, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA were kept. SVGs in Part B were fetched as text and have no `<style>` and no `class=`.
- I re-checked 5 of the 15 Part A files against the live API: licence and width matched in all 5. Part B was not spot-checked.
- **Nobody opened the images.** Look at each when wiring it in.
- 3 questions are marked for rewording, all "Tko je na slici?" portraits: Q58 Gauss, Q59 Descartes, Q99 Pythagoras.
- Check by eye: B7 Mandelbrot (whole set, not a zoom), B8 Königsberg (recoloured Merian plan; make sure it has no caption that gives the answer). Q55's icosahedron SVG has no author in its metadata (CC BY-SA 3.0); the Tomruen PNG is only labelled "Attribution".
- Many portraits are under 640 px, so no picture: Euclid (262 px), Recorde, Mirzakhani. Q68 Getaldić has only a house photo, which gives the answer (Dubrovnik) away to anyone who recognises it (marked "ne").
- Skipped on purpose: Q63 (the base-60 tablets are Babylonian, not Sumerian), Q42 Hipatija (available pictures are painted scenes of her murder with nudity), cube and Platonic-solid pictures (give away Q53, 54, 55, 91, 92), the pyramid picture for Q102.
- Credit is required for CC BY and CC BY-SA files.
